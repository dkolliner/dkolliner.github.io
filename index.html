Daniel Kolliner

I am the Wright Family Assistant Professor of Economics at Kenyon College with interests in political economy and public economics. My research examines how political institutions shape the distribution of public goods and services, and what policies may mitigate distortions in that distribution. I leverage quasi-experimental research designs and creative uses of underused public data sources to estimate causal effects.

Prior to joining Kenyon, I earned my Ph.D. at the University of Maryland. I previously worked as a Research Assistant at the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland and as a Risk Analyst at KeyBank.
